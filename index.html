From the lab toward deployment

During my doctoral and postdoctoral research, I developed advanced materials for energy and environmental applications while collaborating with startups to move ideas from the lab toward real-world deployment.

Most recently at Stanford, I supported commercialization and market-facing efforts for an early-stage technology focused on resource recovery from wastewater and green ammonia production — conducting customer discovery with industrial stakeholders, evaluating deployment constraints, and helping shape strategy for bringing the technology to market.

Earlier, I worked closely with founders and multidisciplinary teams in startup environments, translating complex science into practical solutions and strategic decisions. Alongside my technical work, I’m deeply interested in community building, mental health, and leadership development.

Currently exploring opportunities in consulting, innovation strategy, and sustainability-focused problem solving.
